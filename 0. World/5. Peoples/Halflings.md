# Halflings
Halflings believe luck is real, and that it is not personal but communal: a shared pool that a family or a village holds together. You tend it with small courtesies and small rituals, and you spend it with boasting, greed & needless risk. A Halfling who gambles the household's fortune on pride has not only risked themselves, they have **stolen** from everyone.

In a realm where fortune does sometimes visibly bend in mortals' favor ([[DM Custom Rules#Luck Events|Luck Events]]), this reads less like superstition than like plain observation.

## Rooted & roaming
Halflings are the quiet heart of many [[Luminia|Luminian]] farm villages, and the two kindreds tend luck differently:
- **Stout** halflings keep the *rooted* luck of hearth and harvest
- **Lightfoot** halflings carry a *traveling* luck, sharing it road to road

## Friction
Others hear the constant small rituals and call Halflings superstitious. Halflings in turn quietly distrust the loud and the grasping, the sort who spend the whole room's luck and never think to put any back.
