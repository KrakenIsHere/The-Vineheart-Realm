# Goblins
Goblin culture has no concept of **property**. A stick lying in the road and a tool locked in a shed are equally available if a job needs doing. Goblins take things, use them, and set them down wherever they finish, eat from communal pots, and genuinely cannot make sense of why the [[Peoples#The Core Races|Core Races]] are so angry, and so quick to violence, over objects.

## The word they do have
There is no Goblin word for theft. The nearest one means something closer to **greedy**, and it describes a person who hides things so nobody else can use them, sitting on a tool while the work goes undone. It is close to the worst thing one Goblin can call another, and saying it will start a fight.

## Friction
Goblins living near Core settlements earn a reputation as thieves entirely by behaving normally. They are not stealing, since there is nothing to steal from, and the Core Races are not being unreasonable either, since a lock on a shed is a fairly clear statement. Neither side has ever had the words to explain itself to the other.
