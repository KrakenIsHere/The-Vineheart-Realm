# Tieflings
The infernal mark (horns, tail, solid eyes) arrives unbidden, often to entirely ordinary families, and the realm has never quite known what to make of it. The [[Divine War]] left mortals wary of every strange power, and dark bargains like the [[The Immortal Soul|Immortal Soul]] legend keep the dread of the infernal alive, so a Tiefling learns young that strangers will read an **omen** in their face before they have said a word.

## Deeds over blood
Tiefling culture rejects inherited guilt outright. You are not your blood, not some bargain-making ancestor, not the rumor pinned to your horns. **You are what you do.**

They build found-families out of the similarly cast out, prize self-reliance, and judge others (and themselves) strictly by action rather than reputation.

## Friction
Others project the worst onto the mark and wait for the Tiefling to disprove it. Tieflings, worn down by the assumption, can come across as guarded or prickly, a wall built by a lifetime of being suspected first and known later.
