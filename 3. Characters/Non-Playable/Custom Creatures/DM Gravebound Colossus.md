## Description

The **Gravebound Colossus** is a custom **Undead**, specifically an assembled zombie. Several ogre corpses supply its enlarged body and four arms. Necromancy joins and animates the dead flesh. Horns and black drake scales are grafted onto it; the scales become part of its armored hide, including its hands and stomach.

Necromancy animates the dead flesh directly. The creature is Undead regardless of the types of creatures used in its construction.

## Behavior

It can coordinate its arms and follow simple standing orders. It has no established spellcasting or conversational ability. Its body remains after defeat. It has no regeneration, automatic rebirth, or [[The Soul Bound|Soul bound]] item transformation.

**Multiattack.** The colossus makes three Heavy Weapon attacks, choosing among the weapons it holds. Four arms do not grant four attacks.

**Heavy Weapon.** Melee attack, +9 to hit, reach 10 ft. (about 3 m), one target. On a hit, it deals **2d8 + 6** damage. An axe or sword deals Slashing damage; a heavy improvised club deals Bludgeoning damage. This profile requires no weapon enchantment.

### Malgorth

**Malgorth** is the colossus in [[DM Inverse Impact Characters#Encounter Creatures|Haruto's encounter record]]. **Grave Tyrant** is its title, not its species. It carries an axe, a broad sword, a hooked blade, and an iron door mounted on a handle. The sword lodged in its chest is an old injury to dead tissue; it imposes no additional HP loss or penalty at the start of the encounter.

Its first appraisal shows **168 / 168 HP**. Haruto's amplified push drives it into the counterweight. The failing structure then crushes it. The later window records **0 / 168 HP** and **Defeated**.

### Origins & open details

Malgorth already exists before the Divine War. Its creator, the source of its name and title, and its original orders remain open. Its presence does not establish why the Hanging Halls were built or who controlled them.

Its challenge rating and XP value require a balance pass before use in a campaign. No numerical XP award is assigned to the story encounter.
