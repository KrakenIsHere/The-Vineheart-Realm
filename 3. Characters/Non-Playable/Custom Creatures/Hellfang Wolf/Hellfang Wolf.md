## Description

A **Hellfang Wolf** is a living wolf altered by magic. It has three pairs of red eyes and a heavy jaw with long canine teeth. Its shoulders reach about a human's waist. It remains a flesh-and-blood animal and needs food, water, and sleep.

The name refers to its appearance. It does not identify the wolf as a creature from the Hells.

## Behavior

Hellfang wolves hunt by scent and sound as well as sight. They bite and pull at prey. An injured or trapped wolf can still reach anything near its head.

Their extra eyes do not let them see through walls. A trapped wolf has considerably less room to express its opinion.
