## Description

The **Forest Slime** is a custom **Ooze** preserving the green slimes already written in Chapter 2 of **Inverse Impact**. It is not the published Green Slime hazard.

## Behavior

It pursues Haruto, springs toward him, and clings to his shin. Contact stings through his pants. His light push through a branch launches it into a tree, where it bursts. Other slimes then block the road and drive him into the woods.

These are homebrew mechanics for the existing creature. The established appraisal remains **7 HP**, **AC 8**, and an acidic-contact warning. Challenge rating and XP still require a balance pass.

**Pseudopod.** Melee attack, +0 to hit, reach 5 ft. (about 1.5 m), one target. A hit deals **1 Acid damage**. If the target is Medium or smaller, the slime can cling to its body.

**Clinging Jelly.** Clinging does not impose Grappled or Restrained, and the target can move normally with the slime attached. A creature can use an action to pull it off with a successful DC 8 Strength (Athletics) check. It can also be dislodged by moving or striking the slime. Remaining attached deals no automatic damage; the slime must use Pseudopod again to deal damage. This profile grants no equipment corrosion or lingering acid injury.
