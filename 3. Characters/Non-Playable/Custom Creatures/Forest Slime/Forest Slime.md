## Description

A **Forest Slime** is a small green **Ooze**. Pebbles and bits of bone can be visible inside its body. It flows over uneven ground and gathers itself before springing toward nearby prey.

## Behavior

It follows moving prey and clings to a leg when it reaches one. Contact with its body stings. Several can emerge from the same ditch, making the route past them harder to use.
