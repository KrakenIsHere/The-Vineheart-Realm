# Reference rules

Appearance tables record author reference values. Height and weight are working measurements, not measurements Haruto has made. Exact ages remain open except where the story gives one. Stable features, first-appearance condition, and later changes have separate rows. Select details for a scene according to what Haruto can observe.

The cast includes humans, dwarves, half-elves, wood elves, half-orcs, a rock gnome, a lightfoot halfling, and Mira, who has human and forest gnome parents. Their appearance and species traits are recorded together. Haruto does not automatically know a person's exact ancestry from an ear shape or stature.

The people below use **homebrew NPC profiles**. Their levels follow [[Levels Breakdown#Common Folk|the vault's population scale]] and [[Levels Breakdown#Professional Class|professional scale]]. A trade level supplies the proficiencies listed in its profile. It does not grant an adventuring class or unlisted player-character features. The two wizard-based profiles use the specified caster features and resources.

Use [[Character Creation#Races Breakdown|the vault's species rules]], [[Character Creation#Class Selection Guide|first-level HP]], and [[Dice#Hit Dice by Class|Hit Dice]]. Ability scores are final, with applicable species adjustments already included. Humans have the vault's extra skill rather than automatically receiving additional 2024 origin features. The dwarf profiles use 25 ft. (about 7.5 m) speed. Wood elves use the vault's 30 ft. (about 9 m) speed and its stated terrain benefits. Small character size does not change the class-based recovery die.

For new profiles, HP uses the first die's maximum and the rounded-up average at later levels, plus Constitution at every level: 3 per later d4, or 4 per later d6. Recovery follows [[Gameplay Changes#Rest:|the rest rules]]. HP and slots in the profiles are maximum capacities; track expenditure when a scene establishes it. Haruto's existing resource record remains authoritative. Do not turn previously narrated bruises and cuts into additional full-damage attacks.

Proficiency follows level. Unlisted skills use the appropriate ability modifier without proficiency; each saving-throw column already includes any applicable proficiency. **Expertise** doubles the proficiency part once, where explicitly marked. Tool bonuses use the ability printed in their row; another task can use a different ability with the same proficiency. Familiarity with a tool does not mean the complete kit is carried in every scene. Improvised weapons do not inherit a tool proficiency.

General attack, save, armor, and equipment calculations use the [SRD 5.2.1](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf) and [2024 equipment rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/equipment), with vault overrides taking precedence. **Controlled Blow** is a specific homebrew option in Dren's profile. NPC classless levels use d4 recovery, as Haruto does. Custom creatures retain their own profiles and proficiency bonuses; CR and XP values still awaiting balance remain unassigned.

The court invocation and lasting translation are story-specific ritual effects. They are not freely repeatable uses of an ordinary prepared spell. Mira's **Comprehend Languages** is her normal caster-only spell; it does not give Haruto permanent translation or written Common. Curse assessment, failed suppression, and a possible cure remain governed by [[DM World Implications#The Inverse Impact Skill|the existing Skill rules]]. The NPC profiles do not establish a return route to Earth.

Keep these statistics in the DM reference. Haruto learns only what the scenes and [[DM World Implications#Windows & Appraisal|window rules]] reveal. Dice and recovery bookkeeping remain outside chapter prose.

# Main Characters

## Haruto Minase

**Haruto Minase** is a twenty-two-year-old Japanese man who processes invoices. He falls asleep on a commuter train and is intentionally summoned while alive. He is not resurrected after a heroic death.

His exceptional trait is the bound Skill. His body, knowledge, language limitations, and survival skills remain ordinary. He is neither a trained champion nor an automatic substitute for the realm's existing heroes.

## Princess Elyra Obsidia

**Princess Elyra Obsidia** is a daughter of the reigning Obsidia monarch at the mountain court around 312 BGW. She questions Haruto about his work, opposes further dangerous testing, and presses for an escort and follow-up assistance. She cannot make the council promise help by a definite date.

She appears near Haruto's age. Her exact age, mother's identity, birth order, and place in the succession are not established.

[[Onyxia#The House of Shadowrest|The Shadowrest history]] identifies a marriage to the youngest daughter of the former Obsidia ruling house. It does not identify that daughter as Elyra. Do not give Elyra that marriage or make her a founder of the later dynasty without resolving the chronology and genealogy.

## Nessa

**Nessa** repairs the water gates carrying drainage from the mines above through the old reservoir. She meets Haruto at a service gate in Chapter 4, carrying a lantern, a wrench, and keys. She came down the previous day to replace a band on a leaking pipe. The collapse damages the upper service stair and jams an outlet weight. She shuts the main feed and works to lower the reservoir so she can inspect a lower ledge and a second set of steps around the damaged landing.

Her normal route to the basin workroom crosses the bridge from the upper galleries, follows the aisle beside the scraping skeleton, and descends to the junction and gutter. It avoids the old lift branch and Malgorth's chamber. The bridge falls after she returns to the reservoir side. She recognizes fresh damage, altered water flow, and mechanisms that worked properly before the fall.

She brought the food to the workroom before starting the repair. Water crews use the dry, secure room for meals and rest, with shared bedding and tools. She has not returned since Haruto took the leather pads; she recognizes the equipment itself. His opening through the sealed arch provides a detour back to those supplies. She knows the repeated junctions, corrects his map, and opens the shifted service gate after he explains his curse, asking him to keep his hands away from the controls.

Her exact age, employer, and wider history are not established. She reports that the chamber guard has not left its room onto the service route during their work; this does not establish magical confinement. At the end of Chapter 4, she knows which lower galleries the separate creature's cries come from, but its identity and an open route to the surface remain unconfirmed.

In the Chapter 5 draft, she learns how Haruto defeated the guard and how he reached the dungeon after his summoning and relocation. She uses his opened arch to retrieve food, accepts his coin for the crew's accounts, and lends him the pads and tool. She secures the weight before his amplified impact, checks the rails afterward, and opens the reservoir outlet. She corrects the pad's heel strap after his fall and leads him across the exposed inspection ledge. Both pass a chained ogre zombie without combat and reach a surface work yard. She introduces him to **[[#The worker at the service exit|Bren Harl]]** as someone who is with her. This establishes help and cooperation, not employment or a permanent adventuring party.
