# Main Characters

## Haruto Minase

**Haruto Minase** is a twenty-two-year-old Japanese man who processes invoices. He falls asleep on a commuter train and is intentionally summoned while alive. He is not resurrected after a heroic death.

His exceptional trait is the bound Skill. His body, knowledge, language limitations, and survival skills remain ordinary. He is neither a trained champion nor an automatic substitute for the realm's existing heroes.

### Starting Statistics

Haruto uses a homebrew civilian profile. His **Level 2** follows [[Levels Breakdown#Common Folk|the vault's ordinary adult scale]]. It does not give him an adventuring class.

| Statistic | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Adventuring class | None |
| Ability scores | STR 10, DEX 10, CON 10, INT 10, WIS 10, CHA 10 |
| Maximum **Hit Points** | 9, using **2d8** with no Constitution modifier |
| **Armor Class** | 10, unarmored with no Dexterity modifier |
| **Spell Slots** | None |

**Inverse Impact** is a separate curse and consumes no spell slots. The gloves, cloak, and ring provide no AC bonus. Classifications such as **Catastrophic Pacifist** and **The Gentle Calamity** describe him without changing these statistics.

| Story point | Current HP |
| --- | --- |
| Summoning, Chapter 2 | 9 / 9 |
| After the wolf, Chapter 1 | 5 / 9 |
| After the three soldiers | 4 / 9 |
| After Malgorth | 2 / 9 |

Use these totals to keep later scenes consistent. Minor bruises and descriptive pain do not each require another HP loss. Proper rests and healing use [[Gameplay Changes#Rest:|the vault's rules]].

His eventual disappearance from reliable history, the contradictory accounts of the Gentle Calamity, and his final fate remain to be written.

# Round Characters

# Flat Characters

## Princess Elyra Obsidia

**Princess Elyra Obsidia** is a daughter of the reigning Obsidia monarch at the mountain court around 312 BGW. She questions Haruto about his work, opposes further dangerous testing, and presses for an escort and follow-up assistance. She cannot make the council promise help by a definite date.

She appears near Haruto's age. Her exact age, species, mother's identity, birth order, and place in the succession are not established.

[[Onyxia#The House of Shadowrest|The Shadowrest history]] identifies a marriage to the youngest daughter of the former Obsidia ruling house. It does not identify that daughter as Elyra. Do not give Elyra that marriage or make her a founder of the later dynasty without resolving the chronology and genealogy.

## High Magister Corven

**High Magister Corven** leads the invocation that deliberately summons an extraordinary champion. He arranges examination and physical testing, then reads the appraisal and the court's relocation decision.

He bears responsibility for bringing Haruto into the world. The story does not establish that he knowingly selected a cursed person or created the Skill. His promise to investigate a return route has not produced a method.

His age, species, magical level, temple position, and later fate remain open. His title alone does not assign a level from [[Levels Breakdown]].

## Ordan

**Ordan** is a dwarf responsible for the testing yard and skilled in the mountain city's stonework and supports. He applies the craft belief described in [[Dwarves#The perfect form|the dwarven creed of one true form]], associated with [[Gods#Morlith, The Mountain Lord|Morlith]].

He enforces safety in the testing yard, supplies ordinary leather gloves, and escorts Haruto to the outer watchpost. He returns when damaged machinery requires his attention.

His family, guild, formal rank, level, and later history are not established.


# Background Characters

## The Obsidia monarch
**The Obsidia monarch** is Elyra's father, seen watching the invocation. His personal name and exact title are not given. House Obsidia rules this pre-war court; do not use "king of Onyxia" for him.

## The appraising mage
**The appraising mage** is the young woman who supplies translation and examines the binding. Her name and formal relationship to Corven are not given.
