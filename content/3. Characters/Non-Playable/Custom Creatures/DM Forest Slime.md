## Description

The **Forest Slime** is a custom **Ooze** preserving the green slimes already written in Chapter 2 of **Inverse Impact**. It is not the published Green Slime hazard.

## Behavior

It pursues Haruto, springs toward him, and clings to his shin. Contact stings through his pants. His light push through a branch launches it into a tree, where it bursts. Other slimes then block the road and drive him into the woods.
