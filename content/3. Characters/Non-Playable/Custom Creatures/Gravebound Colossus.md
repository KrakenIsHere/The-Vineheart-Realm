## Description

A **Gravebound Colossus** is an oversized zombie assembled from several ogre corpses. It has four arms with grasping hands. Grafted horns and a covering of black scales give it the appearance of a creature that might once have been alive in that shape.

It was not. The extra arms are part of its construction.

## Behavior

A colossus can wield heavy weapons and follow simple orders to guard a place or attack an intruder. Its movements are coordinated, but it does not need to breathe or react to wounds as a living creature would. A blade through its chest is evidence of a previous fight, not evidence that the fight is over.
