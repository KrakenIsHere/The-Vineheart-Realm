## Description

The **Hellfang Wolf** is a custom **Monstrosity**, a magically altered wolf. Its mutation changes its anatomy and strength. It does not make the animal a Fiend or an Undead.

## Behavior

It attacks with its jaws and follows ordinary animal needs. Its name grants no fire damage, breath weapon, or damage resistance.

**Keen Hearing & Smell.** The wolf has Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.

**Bite.** Melee attack, +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. (about 1.5 m), one target. On a hit, it deals **1d10 + 3** Piercing damage.

### Story encounter

[[DM Inverse Impact Characters#Encounter Creatures|Haruto's encounter record]] places the wolf between two fallen beams before contact. Its jaws can move, but its front legs are pinned. The first appraisal appears when he finds it at the end of Chapter 2, showing **37 / 37 HP**, **AC 14**, and **Restrained**. It warns that the wolf is lethal within reach and recommends keeping his distance. After Haruto launches it through the failing wall in Chapter 1, the contact report updates it to **0 / 37 HP** and **Dead**.

### Origins & open details

The wolf exists before the Divine War. It is not a product of the Godwound. The source of its alteration, whether other Hellfang wolves exist, and whether the changes can be inherited remain open.

Its challenge rating and XP value require a balance pass before use in a campaign. No numerical XP award is assigned to the story encounter.
