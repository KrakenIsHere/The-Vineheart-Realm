# Gnomes
A Gnome may live four or five centuries, and to a Gnome the whole point of so much time is to never run out of **surprise**. Boredom is treated almost as an illness. An unanswered question is a small debt owed to the world, and they will begin projects they cannot possibly finish purely for the joy of the asking.

## Where the questions lead
**Rock** gnomes aim their curiosity at made things: clockwork, tinkering, how it all fits together. **Forest** gnomes aim it at living things: illusions, small beasts & the secrets of green places.

Gnomes are over-represented in [[Memberia|Memberia's]] [[Shieldhaven|Athenaeum]], where curiosity is currency, and where their appetite for *any* answer, even a dangerous one, sometimes leads them toward knowledge better left sealed. The fragments of the [[The Immortal Soul|Immortal Soul]] legend quarantined in the lower stacks are exactly the sort of thing a Gnome cannot leave alone.

## Friction
Others find Gnomes flighty, forever chasing the next question and rarely finishing the last. But a Gnome who has stopped asking has, by their own lights, already begun to die.
