# Dwarves
Dwarves inherited the old creed of [[Gods#Morlith, The Mountain Lord|Morlith]]: every craft, every task & every stone has **one true form** it is meant to reach, and the worker's whole duty is to reach it. A shoddy weld or a "good enough" wall is not merely poor work, it is a small dishonesty, a thing pretending to be what it is not.

## The perfect form
They apprentice for decades and measure a life by what they mastered rather than what they owned. A Dwarf will gladly spend three years on a task a Human would rush in a week, and consider the Human to have wasted the week.

## Mountain & hearth
Dwarves are the backbone of [[Onyxia|Onyxian]] mining and stonework, though their halls reach into every mountain range.
- **Mountain** dwarves pursue the perfect *work*: stone, steel & craft
- **Hill** dwarves pursue the perfect *hearth*: a home, a table, a community done right

## Friction
Others hear a Dwarf's flat "that is done wrong" as arrogance. To the Dwarf it is plain honesty, and staying quiet while you botched the thing would have been the real insult.
