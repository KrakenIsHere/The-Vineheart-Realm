# Elves
Elves do not sleep, they [[Character Creation#Elf|Trance]], and in trance they relive their own memories with all their original color. To an Elf a joy from two centuries ago is not a faded story, it is a place they can return to tonight. So is a grief. So is a betrayal.

## Memory made present
This is why Elves seem unable to move on. They are not brooding, they are simply still *there*, in a moment everyone else let fade long ago. A debt owed to an Elf's grandmother may still, to the Elf who watched it made, feel owed this week.

## Kindreds
- **High Elves** keep the memory of cities, lore & the old arts
- **Wood Elves** keep the memory of the living wild, and are common in the deep forests of [[Arcanum]] and eastern [[Onyxia]]
- **Drow** went below during the [[Divine War]] and stayed. They relive not the world's memory but the *bloodline's*, ruled by matriarchs who hold the longest memories, and wary of a sunlit surface that forgets so easily

## Friction
The short-lived races find Elves aloof and slow to forgive. From the Elf's side, everyone else simply *forgets* too fast, treating the dead as gone and old wrongs as settled, when to the Elf nothing has faded at all.
