# Actual Title
Awoken in Another World: with My Absurd Skill, The Softer I Touch the Harder I Hit, Now I Roam the Dungeon Alone with My Cursed Skill

# Historical Placement

These events take place at approximately **312 BGW** in the southern mountains of **Magnica**, during the growing divine tensions recorded in [[The Timelines#Overall|the overall timeline]]. Magnica is still a single landmass. **[[Onyxia]]** does not exist at this time; it emerges during the [[The Timelines#The New Age|post-war recovery era]]. **BGW** is also a later historical reckoning.

# The Inverse Impact Skill

**Inverse Impact** is the short name for the story and the name of **Haruto Minase's** Skill. The less force he intentionally applies, the greater the impact. Ordinary contact stays safe; the resulting force has a finite ceiling.

## Contact & force

The Skill activates when Haruto deliberately pushes, strikes, squeezes, or presses against a target. He does not need to intend harm or consciously activate it.

Ordinary contact and handling are safe. He can walk, eat breakfast, hold a cup, and wear clothing. Resting a hand on a wall is safe; pushing against it to catch his balance can activate the Skill.

| Force Haruto applies | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Full-force punch or shove | Very little effect |
| Firm pressure | Roughly ordinary effect |
| Light push, tap, or flick | Launches a creature or breaks timber |
| Very gentle pressure | Breaks stone or dislodges a heavy structure |

## Limits

- Judge the force of the whole action. Fear matters when it changes his movement or force.
- One action produces one impact, in the direction he applies force. Held tools carry the effect. A gentle throw can launch an object violently; later collisions do not amplify it again.
- There is a finite ceiling. Zero force produces no impact. His strongest direct impact can wreck part of a room; larger collapses need failing supports or unstable structures.
- His body remains ordinary. The Skill protects him from amplified recoil, but enemies, debris, exhaustion, and ordinary strain can still hurt him. It grants no training, levels, or healing.

## Affection & equipment

**Affection Multiplier** strengthens an active impact when he sincerely cares about its target, within the same ceiling. A friendly voice alone does not qualify. An affectionate pat or kiss can be dangerous; passive contact remains safe.

**Gloves of Delicate Control** soften minor unintended bursts but cannot make deliberate attacks safe. The **Traveler's Cloak** and **Ring of Mild Warmth** provide shelter and warmth. The ring does not prevent hypothermia. The court's failed ward does not establish immunity to every form of suppression. The curse's origin and cure remain open.

## Windows & Appraisal

Windows first appear after summoning. They are private to Haruto and readable in Japanese. Haruto can request his status, including level, class, current and maximum HP, Armor Class, spell slots, and classification. Skill and item messages can reveal existing rules later. The windows do not teach local writing or generate rewards.

A classification describes him; it does not grant a class, levels, defenses, or healing. HP measures his remaining ability to survive injury and combat. Recovery follows [[Gameplay Changes#Rest:|the vault's rest rules]] and healing effects. His brief dungeon nap is too short to complete a rest.

The court appraises him through its own tests. His ordinary abilities, destructive Skill, and failed suppression give it a reason to declare him unfit for royal service and relocate him.

# Historical Effects

The damaged testing yard and dungeon passages may leave repair records, ruins, or legends of **The Gentle Calamity**. Establish their location and survival before adding them to modern histories. The dungeon is original, with no established connection to **The Misty Falls**.

Haruto does not cause the Divine War, the Godwound, or the Sundering, and does not replace the Vineheart Party. Morlith's worship at the court does not establish him as the Skill's creator.

The Divine War erupts more than a century after this story. The relationship between southern Magnica and Onyxia's later northern mountains remains open.

# Chapter Continuity

Chapter 1 introduces Haruto's present situation in the dungeon. Chapter 2 covers his summoning and first tests. Chapter 3 will continue from Malgorth's chamber.

Windows appear after summoning. Later windows reveal additional information. Dungeon collapses follow damaged supports and old structural faults.

# Chapter 3 Ideas

Haruto gets lost while exploring and reaches a dead end. He tries using Inverse Impact to make a way through. What lies beyond, and whether the opening helps him find his bearings, remain open.
